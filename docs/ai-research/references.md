## Open bibliography gaps

Targeted sources are still needed for caption supervision in image/video diffusion training, H3/Krea2 prompt conventions, video frame sampling, human-AI conversational interaction, taxonomy induction and multi-label tagging, face/pose landmark reliability, paired visual reference matching, small/quantized local model comparisons, diffusion-specific sample weighting and checkpoint selection. Adjacent papers above are not direct evidence for these gaps.
