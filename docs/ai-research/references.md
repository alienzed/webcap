## Remaining applicability gaps

The initial 18-topic pass is complete. The following remain open questions, not uncompleted documentation tasks or requirements to reproduce research: direct evidence for the actual small/quantized local models and chat templates; WebCap-specific caption templates, fine-grained visual attributes and frame sampling; face/pose-derived classification reliability; author-guided short-Scene creative specifications; exact H3/Krea 2 LoRA training hyperparameters, exposure and checkpoint selection; and calibration of existing judges and weighting policies. The topic notes identify the corresponding limitations. Provider guidance may describe model-specific requirements but does not establish independent comparative effectiveness. Do not claim a study validates an existing implementation unless the relationship is actually demonstrated.
