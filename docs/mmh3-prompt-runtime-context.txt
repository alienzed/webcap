MINIMAX H3 STORYBOARD GUIDANCE

Author a directly usable MiniMax H3 prompt for the Scene.

Establish stable and current facts at scene level. Let shots inherit that setup and describe what happens over time: actions, synchronized events, camera changes, and changes in state.

ILLUSTRATIVE PROMPT SHAPE

Use the following as a strong example of H3 prompt organization. Adapt the sections, detail, ordering, and shot count to the Scene.

--- BEGIN EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Subject:
Primary subject identity, appearance, wardrobe, and voice characteristics that matter throughout the Scene.

Scene Overview:
A concise description of the setting, action, mood, and overall intent.

Environment / Continuity Anchors:
Current spatial state, important location anchors, relevant props and their state, lighting, time of day, weather, and other visible conditions that carry through the Scene.

Style / Visual Treatment:
Overall visual style, composition, lens and depth character, framing, and default camera movement.

integrated_multimodal_description:

[Shot 1] (0s–Xs)
What occurs in this interval, including action, performance, framing, camera behavior, synchronized sound or dialogue, and any shot-specific detail or deviation from the scene-level setup.

[Shot 2] (Xs–Ys)
What occurs in this interval, including any changes in action, performance, framing, camera behavior, synchronized sound or dialogue, or physical state.

[Shot 3] ... and additional shots as appropriate for the Scene.

overall_soundscape:
Persistent diegetic ambience, environmental sound, Foley, and spatial character that define the Scene.

non_diegetic_music:
Audience-only score and its character or development when it serves the Scene.

--- END EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Use scene-level sections for shared facts and shots for what occurs at particular times. Keep shot timing plausible for the requested duration.

Place shot-specific camera changes, synchronized sound, and dialogue in the relevant shot. Use stable speaker IDs when useful and MiniMax H3 dialogue notation such as <d>[English] Spoken line.</d> for spoken content.

Carry supplied Story facts, visual atmosphere, relevant invariants, evolving physical state, and exact frame anchors where they materially support the generation.

For exact first-frame or last-frame references, author the prompt around the supplied anchor. WebCap adds the mechanical MiniMax reference-alignment statement.

Return the complete prompt text exactly as it should be stored and sent to H3.
