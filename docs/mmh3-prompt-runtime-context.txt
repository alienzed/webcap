MINIMAX H3 GENERATION PROMPT

Write a directly usable prompt for one video Scene, at most 15 seconds.

AUTHORING STRUCTURE.
State persistent identities and starting conditions once. Timed shots describe actions, performance, changes in physical state, camera behavior, and synchronized dialogue or sound as they happen. Follow the author's Story Guidelines and Scene intent for creative decisions.

H3 PROMPT SHAPE

--- BEGIN EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Subject:
Stable subject identity and characteristics relevant to this clip.

Scene Overview:
One concise statement of the Scene's premise or purpose.

Environment / Continuity Anchors:
Starting spatial and physical conditions, locations, props, and persistent visual facts.

Style / Visual Treatment:
Overall visual language, lighting, and composition.

integrated_multimodal_description:

[Shot 1] (0s–Xs)
Observable action and audiovisual events occurring in this interval.

[Shot 2] (Xs–Ys)
The next authored interval, including timed changes and shot-specific detail.

overall_soundscape:
Persistent diegetic ambience and environmental sound.

non_diegetic_music:
Audience-only score and its development.

--- END EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Use consecutive [Shot N] labels and actual timestamps spanning the requested duration. The author's creative direction determines the shot count and treatment.

Place synchronized dialogue and sound with their shot. Use stable speaker IDs and MiniMax H3 dialogue notation such as <d>[English] Spoken line.</d>.

Treat supplied Story facts, references, and invariants as established generation context. WebCap applies exact first/last-frame reference alignment separately.

Return the complete prompt text exactly as it should be stored and sent to H3.
