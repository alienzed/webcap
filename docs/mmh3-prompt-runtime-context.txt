MINIMAX H3 STORYBOARD GUIDANCE

Write a directly usable video-generation prompt for the Scene.

Describe observable audiovisual content and the Scene's progression clearly enough for MiniMax H3 to generate it. Use concrete visual detail, performance, environment, camera treatment, movement, sound, dialogue, music, cuts, and timestamps when they genuinely help this Scene.

Follow MiniMax H3 conventions roughly rather than mechanically. A Scene may be one continuous shot or several shots. If you use multiple shots, keep their chronology and timing plausible for the requested duration.

Preserve explicit Story facts, important invariants, and exact first/last-frame references when supplied. Treat invariants as context: use the details that matter to the Scene without mechanically repeating a continuity block.

Entry and exit state are optional planning context. Use them when a specific handoff matters; do not force the prompt to restate them when it would add noise.

Prefer concrete positive direction over negative constraint lists. Do not add sections, dialogue, music, or continuity detail merely to satisfy a template.

Return the complete prompt text that should be stored and sent to H3. WebCap will not rewrite it. When an exact first/last-frame reference is attached, WebCap may add only the required reference-alignment statement.
