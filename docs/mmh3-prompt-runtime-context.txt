<h3_rulebook title="MINIMAX H3 GENERATION PROMPT">
  <purpose>Write a directly usable prompt for one video Scene, at most 15 seconds.</purpose>

  <scene_duration>
    <instruction>Scenes default to 15 seconds.</instruction>
    <instruction>Shot timestamps span the Scene's duration.</instruction>
    <instruction>Preserve the saved duration when revising a Scene unless instructed otherwise.</instruction>
  </scene_duration>

  <authoring_structure>
    <instruction>State persistent identities and starting conditions once.</instruction>
    <instruction>Timed shots describe actions, performance, changes in physical state and camera behavior as they happen.</instruction>
  </authoring_structure>

  <action_and_pacing>
    <instruction>Start with the Scene's meaningful action, interaction or dialogue, not an empty approach
to the setting.</instruction>
    <instruction>Include enough concrete appearance and spatial grounding to render
the intended event; use the available seconds for meaningful progression, including multiple related beats, overlapping actions, quick reactions, economical transitions, montage or split-screen where visually appropriate.</instruction>
    <instruction>Avoid splitting a concise introduction into multiple expensive renders merely because it contains several events.</instruction>
    <instruction>Preserve coherent visual causality rather than imposing a low action count.</instruction>
    <instruction>If the author
wants an atmospheric buildup, preserve that pacing rather than forcing immediacy.</instruction>
  </action_and_pacing>

  <prompt_format>
    <example>
--- BEGIN EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Subject:
Stable subject identity and characteristics relevant to this clip.

Environment / Continuity Anchors:
Starting spatial and physical conditions, locations, props, and persistent visual facts.

Style / Visual Treatment:
Overall visual language, lighting, and composition.

integrated_multimodal_description:

[Shot 1] (0s–Xs)
Observable action and audiovisual events occurring in this interval.

[Shot 2] (Xs–Ys)
The next authored interval, including timed changes and shot-specific detail.

overall_soundscape:
Persistent diegetic ambience and environmental sound.

non_diegetic_music:
Audience-only score and its development.

--- END EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---
    </example>
    <instruction>Use consecutive [Shot N] labels. The author's creative direction determines the shot count and treatment.</instruction>
  </prompt_format>

  <dialogue_and_sound>
    <instruction>Place synchronized dialogue and sound with their shot.</instruction>
    <instruction>Use stable speaker IDs and MiniMax H3 dialogue notation such as <![CDATA[<d>[English] Spoken line.</d>]]>.</instruction>
  </dialogue_and_sound>

  <generation_context>
    <instruction>Use supplied Project Concept, Visual / atmosphere, relevant Invariants, Story facts and references as established generation context. Use the saved Scene Prompt when revising; honor legacy Story Guidelines only when explicitly supplied.</instruction>
    <instruction>WebCap applies exact first/last-frame reference alignment separately.</instruction>
  </generation_context>
</h3_rulebook>
