# MiniMax H3 Illustrative Prompt Shape

This file mirrors the canonical authoring example used by the Director.

It is an authoring example, not a storage schema. Director may adapt section detail, ordering, and shot count to the Scene. WebCap stores Director-authored prompt text unchanged.

--- BEGIN EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Subject:
Primary subject identity, appearance, wardrobe, and voice characteristics that matter throughout the Scene.

Scene Overview:
A concise description of the setting, action, mood, and overall intent.

Environment / Continuity Anchors:
Current spatial state, important location anchors, relevant props and their state, lighting, time of day, weather, and other visible conditions that carry through the Scene.

Style / Visual Treatment:
Overall visual style, composition, lens and depth character, framing, and default camera movement.

integrated_multimodal_description:

[Shot 1] (0s–Xs)
What occurs in this interval, including action, performance, framing, camera behavior, synchronized sound or dialogue, and any shot-specific detail or deviation from the scene-level setup.

[Shot 2] (Xs–Ys)
What occurs in this interval, including any changes in action, performance, framing, camera behavior, synchronized sound or dialogue, or physical state.

[Shot 3] ... and additional shots as appropriate for the Scene.

overall_soundscape:
Persistent diegetic ambience, environmental sound, Foley, and spatial character that define the Scene.

non_diegetic_music:
Audience-only score and its character or development when it serves the Scene.

--- END EXAMPLE PROMPT SHAPE ---

Scene-level sections establish shared defaults. Shots inherit them and describe what occurs at particular times.

Exact first/last-frame modes may also receive WebCap-owned MiniMax alignment syntax derived from the selected reference roles.
