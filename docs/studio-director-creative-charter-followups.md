# Creative-charter follow-ups — prepared, not executed

These are separate experiments, not additional conditions or tuning inside the
three-way PR #1102 comparison. The current evidence can motivate disentangling
compactness, coherent conception and useful initiative; it cannot identify a
universally winning phrase. A bounded one-sentence addition check was subsequently
executed, and the author requested applying that limited Classic refinement;
see the [actual results](studio-director-creative-charter-results.md). Broader
preference and matched-render evidence remain useful for stronger quality claims.
Small-model groups remain relative comparisons.

## 1. Phrase ablation

First select a promising full charter treatment from actual whole-contribution
review. Freeze its assembled role, functional contracts, H3 toolkit/reference,
tasks and schemas in a new manifest. Keep frozen D as an external benchmark.
Compare the full treatment with a version removing **one conceptual unit**;
the remaining wording, placement and punctuation stay identical. Record the
exact textual delta and hashes. Test units in separate arms rather than rewriting
several ideas at once:

| Unit | Existing charter phrase to isolate | Creative question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initiative and judgment | `confident choices, strong artistic judgment and vivid audiovisual specificity` | Does it improve consequential, well-chosen invention? |
| Whole experience | `Envision the work as a fully realized experience` | Does it strengthen audiovisual conception beyond listing ingredients? |
| Relationships | `give its chosen elements coherent form and relationships` | Do image, action, time and sound work together more convincingly? |
| Author intention and open space | `Build on the author’s deliberate choices, and invent purposefully where the vision is open.` | Does it distinguish commitments from opportunities while preserving ambition? |
| Technique serving experience | `Let the intended experience determine composition, performance, movement, stillness, editing and sound.` | Does formal variety follow intent rather than a habitual cinematic template? |
| Collaboration | `Collaborate naturally and proportionately.` | Does it improve the depth and form of the response to the current request? |

The table is a plan for controlled removals, not six new directives. When a
removal crosses a semicolon or list boundary, specify the minimal grammatical
join before freezing; never improve the rest of the sentence at the same time.
The author-fidelity/open-space sentence is initially one unit because removing
only fidelity could change the balance of initiative. A later split would be
a distinct experiment if a meaningful effect warrants it.

Use fresh development briefs spanning Chat, flexible Projects, restrained and
expansive Scenes, with repeated matched trials. Start with the whole-experience
and relationships units if human review corroborates the local coherence signal;
otherwise defer them. Select on whole contributions, not phrase reproduction or
literal totals. Use **new held-out cases** after shortlisting; the current study's
holdouts are consumed evidence. Keep each model's results separate. Collect
arm-blind human preferences and independent Codex notes before keys are opened.
Report tradeoffs and uncertainty rather than announcing a vocabulary winner.

## 2. Contrasting examples

After selecting a stable textual treatment, compare it unchanged with the same
treatment plus **two author-approved examples**. This is an example experiment,
not a simultaneous phrase ablation. No examples are currently approved or added
to the live experiment; an attractive model-generated draft is not approval.

Choose one expansive, ambitious audiovisual work and one deliberately restrained
work. Both should exhibit imaginative decisions, a coherent experience, fidelity
to intentional choices, and collaboration suited to the task. Their quality
should come from different forms and textures, rather than the same camera,
lighting or emotional formula. Use subjects outside every current evaluation
brief. Give each only a compact explanation of the transferable creative decision.

Keep the functional/H3 contracts identical. Record the exact examples, placement,
input length, output length and completion behavior. Judge fresh Chat, Project
and Scene contributions, including distant subject matter; an example benefiting
only a close imitation is weak evidence of transfer. Watch for borrowed imagery,
house style, unwanted narrative arcs and more text without stronger decisions.
If the first pair helps, a later independently approved contrasting pair can
test whether the benefit depends on those particular examples. No example is
promoted into production through this experiment.

## A different strategy: intention contrasts

A separate future test could hold the subject matter steady while changing the
author's intended experience, then change the subject while retaining that intent.
For example, compare generous comedy with austere contemplation, or dense kinetic
collectivity with an intentional held moment. Ask blind humans whether the
Director makes meaningfully different, well-judged artistic choices and preserves
what the author deliberately fixes. This tests abstract responsiveness directly,
rather than accumulating checks for particular scene ingredients. Keep this suite
separate from clause/example selection so it can test transfer honestly.

## Production boundary

The further ablation/example designs are prepared, with no additional calls queued.
PR #1102's original three-way comparison remains fixed. The separate bounded
coherence addition is already tested and applied to Classic at the author's
request; this is not validation of a universal charter winner. For profile
transfer, compare each existing profile against its own treatment with its
personality paragraph preserved verbatim. Render-quality claims require actual
matched outputs; ComfyUI was unavailable during this work. Optional preference
workbooks are not an outstanding requirement for the author. No larger model is
a prerequisite for useful relative experiments or an ambitious creative approach.
