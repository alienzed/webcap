# Cleaned-profile comparison: 18-call practical screen

The later [fixed final comparison](studio-director-unified-final-results.md)
found mixed Scene reliability and recommends retaining Classic v3 for production.
This earlier screen's observations remain intact; cleaned Current was its
practical shortlist choice, not a promoted default.

## Scope and outcome

This follow-up compared all four existing postures after shared editorial
cleanup. The earlier 249 charter calls compared Classic/D treatments; they did
not establish a winner among the four profiles.

**Practical shortlist result: cleaned Current is the strongest general-purpose
choice in this small sample. A second permanent profile did not earn its place.**
This is Codex's qualitative judgment, not a human preference rating or a
statistically validated winner. No new rendering evidence was collected.

The author's subsequent Artist + Mixing Engineer request requires keeping
production guidance and existing profiles unchanged during that investigation.
The uncommitted consolidation was therefore set aside. Production remains the
previous Classic v3/four-profile implementation; the cleaned candidates and all
receipts remain available for comparison. No saved Projects, Scenes, guidance
overrides or local app pause settings were changed by these inference calls.

## Fixed budget and method

- 12 Gemma calls: four cleaned profiles × three fresh briefs (Chat, Project, Scene).
- Six Nullguard calls: Current and Render-focused × three different held-out briefs.
- One trial per cell. **18 actual calls, all completed; no retries or extra probes.**
- Same tasks, schemas, technical H3 reference and per-operation sampling within
  each model group. Only the profile stance differed within a group.
- Profile identities were hidden during individual response review. Unscored
  observations were saved and hashed before assignment keys were opened.
  The analyst authored the candidates and knew model/scenario identities.

Models: `remote-1::mo-shakib/gemma4-e4b-uncensored:q4_k_m` and
`remote-1::h4rithd/nullguard:latest`.

Cleanup retained the WebCap field-definition paragraph verbatim, shortened
overlapping creativity/collaboration directions, and removed repeated technique
catalogues and vague density pressure. Roles fell from 2,479–2,566 characters
to 1,462–1,557; the shared toolkit fell from 1,375 to 547. These are character
counts, not measured token savings. The exact candidate wording is retained in
[packages.json](../experiments/studio_guidance/profile_cleanup/packages.json).

## What actually happened

| Stage | Observation | Decision consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gemma Chat | All four were long and affirming, with closely related neon/distance/blackout conceptions. Imaginative gave the preferred concrete recommendation. | Little evidence of four distinct conversational personalities. |
| Gemma Project | All four supplied title, concept, style and invariants. Classic's developed style best accommodated varying moods; Current was a close alternative. | Useful Project material across postures; extra prose was not a decisive advantage. |
| Gemma Scene | Render-focused gave one fixed-camera interval and the most faithful complete progression. Current used three labeled intervals while describing a held camera. Imaginative explicitly pushed the camera despite the author's fixed-camera choice. | Current and Render-focused advanced: broad usefulness and a concrete adherence distinction. |
| Nullguard Chat | Both supported extravagant comic ambition. Current's concrete comic details were slightly preferred. | Render-focused did not demonstrate a necessary alternate creative personality. |
| Nullguard Project | Both returned useful title/concept/invariants but omitted requested style. Current made a clearer recurring prop choice and added a useful acoustic idea. | Profile changes did not cure field omissions. Partial material remains useful. |
| Nullguard Scene | Both preserved 14 seconds, three intervals, the initial dark dome, the transformation and exact spoken words. Current's authored camera movement and material-linked sound were slightly preferred. | The Render-focused advantage did not carry through this check. |

Scene dialogue notation remained imperfect, particularly language markers/stable
speaker IDs. In the single-shot case, multiple labels alone were treated as
ambiguous continuous-shot treatment, not proof of camera cuts. An explicit push
was a concrete fixed-camera violation. Recurring nostalgic imagery, genre
defaults and agreement-heavy Chat persisted across profiles.

## Limits and the next baseline

There are no repeats, and model identity is confounded with development versus
held-out briefs. Small differences can be sampling variation. The study does not
show that cleanup improves the original wording, isolate a winning phrase, prove
general creative superiority, or establish actual H3 renderability.

For the next isolated study, use **cleaned Current as the single-pass baseline**.
Its exact role/toolkit are frozen in the candidate file. Compare one creative
draft followed by faithful technical refinement, with the initial draft retained
as evidence. The four production profiles remain available during that study.
