# Cleaned-profile selection — bounded protocol

The author requested one strong Director, or two at most, selected by testing
after editorial cleanup. Earlier charter calls compared Classic/D treatments;
they did not compare the four product profiles.

All four candidates share the same shortened role and H3 capability wording.
The WebCap field-definition paragraph is retained verbatim. Each candidate
keeps its existing posture: versatile, editorial, imaginative or production.
The technical H3 authoring reference, operation tasks, schemas and sampling are
unchanged. Production guidance remains untouched during selection.

## Fixed budget and selection

Maximum **18 actual model calls**, with no automatic retries or extra probes:

1. Four cleaned profiles × three fresh development briefs (Chat, Project, Scene)
   × one trial on the previously used Gemma model: 12 calls.
2. Select two finalists from anonymous output cards. Save qualitative Codex notes
   and their hashes before opening the assignment key.
3. Two finalists × three different held-out briefs × one trial on Nullguard:
   six calls. Record separate anonymous Codex observations before opening keys.

Selection prioritizes usable field delivery, the author's deliberate choices,
and feasible H3 structure, followed by imagination, artistic judgment, audiovisual
coherence and natural collaboration. More text is not a quality signal. Retain
useful partial material and identify omissions without pretending it is unusable.
Favor one default; retain a second only for a concrete distinct benefit.

This is a small practical screen with no repeats and model/split confounding;
it cannot establish statistical superiority, cleanly attribute changes between
models, prove that cleanup improves the old wording, or validate rendered quality.
No human preference scores are requested or invented. The existing historical
experiments and their controls stay unchanged. Exact responses/keys remain in
ignored local runs; commit sanitized findings and candidate wording only.
